# Demand gaps: seeds and competitors

Read by `ops/weekly-report/demand.mjs` (Monday report, STEP 3B). It finds searches people make that the site has
no page or backlog idea for, using free sources: Google autocomplete (Australian results) for the seeds below,
and the competitors' sitemaps. The Monday routine may edit this file: add seeds when a new topic area opens up,
add a competitor it keeps seeing rank for our topics, and add rejected ideas to Ignore so they stop coming back.

Every candidate still has to pass `ops/compliance-checklist.md` before it becomes a guide idea: seeds and
suggestions are what people search for, not topics we will necessarily cover.

Format: one `- ` item per line. Competitors are `name | sitemap URL | regex a page URL must match (optional)`.
Six seeds are used each week, rotating through the list.
