# Compliance Checklist — run on EVERY draft before presenting to Adam

Machine-checkable pre-publish rules for psychedelicretreats.com.au. Any automation producing content MUST evaluate each item and either fix failures or flag them prominently in its summary. When in doubt, flag — never guess on compliance.

## Hard rules (fail = do not present without a prominent flag)
1. **No substance + condition + benefit in promotional framing.** "Psilocybin treats depression" ❌. "Researchers at X reported Y in a trial of psilocybin for depression [source]" ✅ (news/informational framing, attributed, sourced).
2. **No efficacy or outcome claims** for any prescription substance (psilocybin, MDMA, ketamine, esketamine, cannabis) — including in quotes, testimonials, or republished material. No before/after stories about therapeutic outcomes.
3. **Nothing that promotes or facilitates illegal activity in Australia.** No underground retreats, no sourcing info, no domestic-illegal offerings — ever, regardless of who asks.
4. **International retreat content** must state the activity is legal in the host jurisdiction (not Australia) and include "Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice."
5. **Australian clinic content = neutral mode only:** name, location, service category, contact, source link. No pricing, no CTA language, no adjectives, no images beyond a logo.
6. **Required blocks present:** medical disclaimer on articles; legal-status notice on substance pages; Lifeline 13 11 14 pointer on mental-health-adjacent pages.
7. **Affiliate links** are `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and the page carries the affiliate-disclosure notice.

## Quality rules (fix before presenting)
8. Every factual claim has a source URL (in frontmatter `sources[]` or inline).
9. Australian spelling; prices in AUD; dates unambiguous; `lastReviewed`/`updatedAt` set.
10. **Publication routing** (revised 2026-08-06 — supersedes "nothing publishes directly"). Automation may publish directly to main, going live immediately, ONLY for: Australian breathwork listings, Australian integration-practitioner listings, neutral-mode Australian clinic listings, and non-substantive maintenance edits (updatedAt bumps, corrected URLs, last-verified updates). Everything else lands as a PR titled "[verify before merge]". ESCALATION — regardless of category, it goes to a PR if it carries any TODO-ADAM, any vetting field resting on less than an unambiguous operator statement, any unresolved compliance flag, any legality question, any unverified claim about a named individual's credentials or conduct, any substance (psilocybin, ayahuasca, MDMA, ketamine, esketamine, cannabis), any destination page, any news post, or any substantive correction. When uncertain, it does not qualify — open a PR. Full routing spec in `ops/routine-prompt.md`.
11. Tone: informational, neutral, safety-first. Banned framing: "treat", "cure", "heal", "medicine that works", hype language.
