## What to expect

Oregon's model is different from a residential retreat: services are structured as individual sessions at a licensed service centre rather than a multi-day live-in program. The Oregon Health Authority describes a three-part structure — one or more preparation sessions (intake, safety planning and informed consent), an administration session at the service centre where the psilocybin product is purchased and consumed on-site with a licensed facilitator present, and integration session support afterwards. Administration sessions commonly run several hours; group sizes, dose options and pricing vary by centre, so check each listing's vetting checklist and the operator's own materials directly.

## Logistics from Australia

There are no direct flights from Australia to Oregon — typical routings reach Portland via Los Angeles, San Francisco or Vancouver, with total travel time in the order of 17–24 hours door-to-door. Some service centres are in regional cities (for example Ashland, in southern Oregon), which adds a connecting flight or a substantial drive from Portland. Standard US entry requirements apply to Australian passport holders.
