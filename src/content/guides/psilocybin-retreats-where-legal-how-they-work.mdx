"Psilocybin retreat" covers a wide range of actual programs. This guide describes the structure most legal operators follow, and the main ways they differ, so you have some context before you compare specific listings. To see every psilocybin retreat in the directory, with the legal position in each country, go to our [psilocybin retreats page](/retreats/psilocybin/).

## The typical program shape

Across the Netherlands and Jamaica operators we've reviewed for this directory, a common pattern shows up: a multi-week program (often 7–11 weeks in total) built around a much shorter in-person retreat (typically 3–6 days). The weeks before the retreat are usually virtual: preparation calls, sometimes a group cohort, and a screening and intake process. The in-person retreat itself centres on one or two guided group ceremonies, supported by facilitators, with structured time for integration before you leave. After you return home, several weeks of virtual integration support is common.

So the few days on site are usually the middle of the program, not the whole of it. This "long program, short retreat" structure isn't universal. Some operators run shorter, retreat-only formats. But it's common enough among the larger, more established operators that it's worth expecting as the default and noting when a program departs from it.

## What actually varies between operators

**Screening rigour.** Some operators publish a detailed, multi-step medical and psychological screening process with an explicit list of contraindications (psychosis, suicidal ideation, dissociative disorders and recent psychiatric hospitalisation are common exclusions). Others describe screening only in general terms. This is one of the six items on our vetting checklist for a reason: check what's actually stated rather than assuming.

**Facilitator model.** Programs differ in whether they use a Western clinical or therapeutic facilitation team, a traditional or Indigenous-led model, or a blend (less common in the Netherlands and Jamaica specifically, more common for ayahuasca). Check named facilitator credentials where they're published.

**Emergency protocol detail.** The strongest operators we've reviewed publish specifics: an onsite medical professional, a named nearby clinic, an evacuation plan. Weaker disclosure just says "trained staff available", which tells you less than it sounds like.

**Price and what's included.** Published prices in this category vary widely, roughly the equivalent of AUD $6,000 to $14,000 among the operators we've reviewed, before flights. What's bundled (private vs. shared rooms, the length of the virtual program, integration calls) varies enough that comparing headline prices alone is misleading. See our cost guide for a fuller breakdown.

## A note on truffles vs. mushrooms

If you're researching Netherlands operators yourself, you'll see "truffles" and occasionally loose references to "mushrooms" used inconsistently across marketing copy and third-party write-ups. The legal distinction is specific and real (see our legal guide), so if an operator's own materials aren't precise about which one they use, ask them directly before booking rather than assuming.
