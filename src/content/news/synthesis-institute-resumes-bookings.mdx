Synthesis Institute, a Netherlands-based legal psilocybin-truffle retreat operator, underwent a publicly reported financial collapse in March 2023. As of our research for this directory, it appears to have resumed operating, with 2026 retreat dates live on its booking system.

## Background

The 2023 collapse was reported by TechCrunch, Willamette Week, and Psychedelic Alpha at the time. Coverage from those outlets describes the Netherlands retreat entity (Synthesis Institute B.V.) filing for bankruptcy, and its Oregon-based sister company (Synthesis Digital LLC, which ran the Psychedelic Practitioner Training) also shutting down, leaving enrolled students in limbo.

## What we found this run

Multiple press accounts of the 2023 collapse (TechCrunch, Willamette Week, Psychedelic Alpha) report that the Psychedelic Practitioner Training was subsequently acquired by a new company, The Resurgence Training Institute, Inc., which now operates the training — and, per current retreat-booking listings, the retreat business — under the continued "Synthesis Institute" name.
