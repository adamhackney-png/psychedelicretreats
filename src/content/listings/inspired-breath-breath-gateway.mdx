Inspired Breath runs "The Breath Gateway", a substance-free, 7-day residential group breathwork retreat in New South Wales. The operator's retreat page describes daily guided breathwork sessions, integration practices, group process and teaching, in small groups with two breathwork teachers present at all times. It also serves as an entry point to the operator's Breathwork Practitioner Training. The retreat page currently shows March 2027 as the next date.

Inspired Breath is led by Phil Morey (founder) and Suzanne Zankin (senior teacher). The Australian Breathwork Association's public member directory lists Philip Morey as a Professional Member and a member of the ABA committee (checked 10 November 2026). The operator's own site describes him as an ABA Accredited Trainer and says its training is endorsed by the Association; the directory does not show trainer status.

No price, group-size figure or medical-screening information is published on the operator's retreat page; applications are made through the operator's online booking link.
