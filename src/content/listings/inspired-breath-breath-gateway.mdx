**Update (18 November 2026):** the operator's retreat page now lists The Breath Gateway as an 8-day retreat held near Totnes, Devon, England, in July 2027. No Australian date for the retreat is shown. The operator's separate training page lists its 7-day Stage 1 practitioner training in Canberra, with the date shown inconsistently (both "March 2027" and "November 7–13, 2026").

Inspired Breath, an Australian breathwork training operator, runs "The Breath Gateway", a group breathwork retreat (conscious connected breathing; no substances are involved). The operator's retreat page describes daily guided breathwork sessions, integration practices, group process and teaching, in small groups with two breathwork teachers present at all times. It also serves as an entry point to the operator's Breathwork Practitioner Training.

Inspired Breath is led by Phil Morey (founder) and Suzanne Zankin (senior teacher). The Australian Breathwork Association's public member directory lists Philip Morey as a Professional Member and a member of the ABA committee (checked 10 November 2026). The operator's own site describes him as an ABA Accredited Trainer and says its training is endorsed by the Association; the directory does not show trainer status.

No price, group-size figure or medical-screening information is published on the operator's retreat page; applications are made through the operator's online booking link.
