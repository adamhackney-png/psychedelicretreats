Sophrodelic is a small Jamaica-based practice that designs private psilocybin sessions and retreats for individuals and couples. Its name combines "sophrology" and "psychedelics", and the operator describes its approach as pairing psilocybin sessions with sophrology and client-centred (Rogerian) therapy techniques. Single-day sessions take place in Kingston; private retreats run from 3 to 10 days, and the operator says retreat locations depend on the length and the client's wishes, with past retreats held in Kingston and Treasure Beach. The site also describes 6- and 8-day group retreat packages in Treasure Beach.

The published process has four steps: at least one discovery session (online or in person) before booking, which covers goals, physical and mental health and current medication; further preparation sessions in the days before dosing; the psilocybin session itself, with the dose and form (tea, chocolate or capsules) agreed beforehand; and an integration session the same evening or the next morning. Retreat packages include airport transfers, accommodation and on-site meals, with off-site meals, massage and local activities available as add-ons.

The practice was founded by Bénédicte (Ben) Mannix, who works in French and English. Her biography on the site says she has facilitated psychedelic sessions for more than 15 years, worked as a host and facilitator with MycoMeditations in Jamaica from 2017 to 2021 and as a team leader with Silo Wellness in Jamaica from 2022 to 2023, and holds sophrology practitioner diplomas from the Sophrology Academy (UK). The operator's team page also lists a pharmacist and a registered nurse.

Published base pricing (in USD) is $750 per person for a single-day session in Kingston, and, per person for private retreats, $1,800 for 3 days (approx. <AudPrice usd={1800} />), $3,600 for 6 days (approx. <AudPrice usd={3600} />), $4,800 for 8 days (approx. <AudPrice usd={4800} />) and $6,000 for 10 days (approx. <AudPrice usd={6000} />), with extra days at $600 each. The operator states experiences are fully refundable when cancelled 60 or more days before the start date and 50% refundable up to 30 days before.

This program is held in Jamaica, where psilocybin is not a prohibited substance. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
