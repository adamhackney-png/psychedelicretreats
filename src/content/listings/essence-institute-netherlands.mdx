Essence Institute runs a 3-day group psilocybin-truffle retreat in the Netherlands, which it calls the Core Retreat: an arrival and intention-setting day, a ceremony day, and a day of integration work before departure. The program includes bodywork, yoga, meditation and breathwork alongside the ceremony, with vegan meals and accommodation at a retreat centre. The operator also lists a retreat for returning participants, a women's retreat and private retreats. Its site names retreat centres in the central and eastern Netherlands.

The operator says it was established in 2019. Its team page names Derk Mulder (described as a psychologist and coach) as founder and Willem Fonteijn (described as a clinical psychologist and psychotherapist) as co-founder, alongside team members described as a psychiatric nurse, a clinical psychologist, breathwork and bodywork coaches and a physiotherapist. The site states there is one facilitator for every five participants, and a minimum age of 25.

On legality, the site states: "In the Netherlands, it is legal to purchase and consume psilocybin in the form of 'magic truffles'." The operator states that "a personal intake and screening are always part of the process", that participants need to be in stable physical and mental health, and that its retreats are not a replacement for medical or psychological treatment. It displays Google reviews on its own site.

Published pricing at the time of review was around €2,050 for a shared room or €2,350 for a single room, including 21% VAT, truffles, meals and accommodation; travel to the venue is not included. No retreat dates were published on the pages reviewed (11 November 2026).

This program is held in the Netherlands, where psilocybin truffles are legal to sell and possess. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
