Melbourne Breathwork is a small team of facilitators running substance-free group breathwork workshops in the Holotropic Breathwork tradition developed by Stanislav and Christina Grof, with facilitators trained through Holotropic Breathwork International (Grof Transpersonal Training). Formats have included one-day and two-day workshops in Melbourne and men's workshops. At the time of review (11 November 2026) the site listed a two-day workshop in Hawthorn on 11–13 December 2026 (Friday evening opening, then Saturday and Sunday), and linked to two five-day Holotropic Breathwork training modules in Tarraleah, Tasmania, in May 2027.

The team also offers post-workshop integration sessions through affiliated practitioners, including the Wild Heart Psychotherapy practice, and is connected to the CPACT (Certificate of Psychedelic and Contemplative Therapies) training program run with the Anam Cara Centre.

The December 2026 two-day workshop is published at $580 full price / $540 concession. The one-day Men's Holotropic Breathwork Day workshop (January 2026) was published at $295 full price / $265 concession.
