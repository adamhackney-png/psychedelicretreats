Entheogenesis runs a 3-day group psilocybin-truffle retreat in the Netherlands, which it calls The Macro Journey. The first day covers arrival, a cacao ceremony, breathwork and a fire ritual; the second day is a full-dose truffle ceremony accompanied by live music; the third day begins integration. Before the retreat, participants get a preparation guide, a preparation call and an online workshop; afterwards there is an integration guide, a 1:1 integration coaching session and a group call about three weeks later. The operator also runs a shorter microdose, breathwork and cacao event (The Micro Journey).

Retreats are held at one of three venues: AVANI in Helvoirt, 't Zonnehuis in Loenen (Veluwe) and The Place To Be in Zelhem. Groups are 16 to 18 participants, with a stated maximum of 18. Retreats are run in English. The operator's dosing note says it generally serves 5 to 15 g of fresh truffles, with an optional second round.

The founding team named on the site is Kasper van der Meulen, Anna Catharin Heil and Victoria Etherington. Their bios, which are the operator's own, describe breathwork teaching, more than 17 years in ceremony facilitation, and psychedelic integration coaching. Joining starts with an application form and an intake call. The site lists contraindications (including pregnancy, psychiatric history and psychiatric medication) and asks applicants to raise any that apply. It also says that "due to privacy rules, we cannot ask you to share your medical history/file with us." The homepage shows the operator's Google reviews.

On legality, the site states: "The 'magic truffle' containing psilocybin is 100% legal in the Netherlands." Published pricing at the time of review was €1,249 excluding 21% Dutch VAT, covering the programme, ceremony, accommodation, vegetarian meals and the 1:1 integration session; a private ensuite room costs €250 extra (excl. VAT), subject to availability. One scholarship place per retreat is offered at €200 (excl. VAT). Listed dates were 5–7 March 2027 (AVANI, Helvoirt), 25–27 June 2027 (women only, The Place To Be, Zelhem) and 17–19 September 2027 ('t Zonnehuis, Loenen), as reviewed on 22 November 2026.

This program is held in the Netherlands, where psilocybin truffles are legal to sell and possess. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
