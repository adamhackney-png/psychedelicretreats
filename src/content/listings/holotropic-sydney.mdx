Holotropic Sydney runs non-residential weekend Holotropic Breathwork workshops in Sydney, with the next listed workshop scheduled for 7–8 November 2026 at a Strathfield location, run as a "double" format across both days. The group also lists five-day retreats, with one facilitator conducting retreats in Tasmania.

Three facilitators are named on the site: Lila Pesa, described as an integrally trained transpersonal psychotherapist specialising in trauma recovery with 13 years' experience; Vicky Nicholson, who the site states supports Grof Transpersonal Training's programs in Australia and the US; and Nikita Pochuev. Holotropic Breathwork is a drug-free breathing practice developed by Stanislav and Christina Grof; sessions pair breathers with sitters under facilitator guidance.

Workshop pages state that participants must complete a confidential medical form before attending, as Holotropic Breathwork has some contraindications, and publish workshop pricing (a recent two-day workshop was listed at AUD $595 full / $545 concession, with a $100 deposit).

Breathwork involves no controlled substances and is legal throughout Australia. This is not medical advice; people with cardiovascular or other significant health conditions should seek advice from a qualified health professional before participating.
