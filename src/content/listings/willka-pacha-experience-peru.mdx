Willka Pacha Experience (also written Willkapacha Experience) is a Cusco-based operator founded by a couple, Wayra and T'ika, who describe themselves as part of a lineage of Andean paccos (ceremonial specialists). Its office is in San Blas, Cusco, and ayahuasca retreats are held at a retreat house in the Sacred Valley near Urubamba, with private transport from Cusco hotels included. Ceremonies are led by Wayra Eloy Quispe Ccorahua ("Maestro Wayra"), who the operator says was born in Acomayo and learned Andean ceremonial practice from his parents and grandparents. The site states he speaks English, Spanish and Quechua, and that retreats are run in English.

The 3-day, 2-night program includes two evening ayahuasca ceremonies, a conversation with the maestro about intentions on arrival, integration conversations on the following mornings and a closing gratitude offering to Pachamama. The operator says this retreat is limited to six participants (minimum two). Accommodation is in private or double rooms at the retreat house; light breakfasts and fruit are included, and Cusco hotel nights, other meals and tips are not. Private ceremonies can be arranged on request.

The operator also runs a 1-night program with one ayahuasca ceremony, a 5-day program with three ayahuasca ceremonies, a 7-day program combining three ayahuasca ceremonies with one wachuma (San Pedro) ceremony and a flower bath, and a 7-day option that adds a visit to Machu Picchu. Daytime wachuma ceremonies run as one- and two-day programs in the Cusco area. Alongside these it offers Andean ceremonies (despacho offerings, crystal and coca-leaf ceremonies) and treks such as the Inca Trail and Ausangate, some of which include a wachuma ceremony.

The operator states that at least one first-aid-trained assistant is on site, with emergency oxygen and medications available, and that the retreat house is near a 24-hour medical clinic with emergency response teams. Its FAQ asks participants to disclose any medication, noting that some, such as antidepressants, can be a problem with ayahuasca; some program pages list a "basic medical check". The site links to the operator's TripAdvisor page.

As of November 2026 the operator's site lists the 3-day program from $590 per person. The program page shows a dollar sign without naming the currency; other pages on the site quote prices in US dollars. Other ayahuasca programs are listed from $275 (1 night) to $1,690 (7 days with Machu Picchu). A 50% deposit or full payment secures a place, with a full refund for cancellations made a month or more before the start date.

This program is held in Peru, where traditional ayahuasca and San Pedro (wachuma) ceremony is legal and culturally protected (see our Peru destination guide). Ayahuasca (which contains DMT) and San Pedro (which contains mescaline) are prohibited substances in Australia. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
