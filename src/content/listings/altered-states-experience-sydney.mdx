Altered States Experience runs a 4-day, 3-night residential breathwork retreat, the Altered States Immersion, at The Oasis, a private coastal property at One Mile Beach, Port Stephens NSW, roughly two and a half hours north of Sydney by car. Intakes are capped at 20 participants, with private cabins and meals included. The published program combines conscious connected breathwork with live music, a firewalk, an ice bath, bodywork and group sessions, with preparation before arrival and a 28-day live-guided integration program afterwards (group breathwork sessions, Q&A calls, guided recordings and journalling prompts). The operator also runs shorter breathwork workshops in Sydney, and describes weekend versions of the retreat. Retreat dates are sent on registration rather than published on the retreat page.

The founder and lead facilitator, Gene Kelly, is described on the site as a Rebirthing breathwork facilitator, holistic health coach and firewalk facilitator. The site also names a lead musician (Joshua Tree) and a bodywork practitioner (Clara Marri) on the facilitation team.

The operator states that the retreat is "fully legal and drug-free". Its FAQ says breathwork is not recommended for some health conditions and asks people with severe cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled epilepsy, recent surgery, pregnancy, active psychosis or any other concern to contact the team before booking. This is not medical advice; people with significant health conditions should seek advice from a qualified health professional before participating.
