The School of Breathwork runs a substance-free, 7-day residential group breathwork immersion (six nights, 12 breathwork sessions) at Gymea Retreat near Uki, in the Byron Bay hinterland, founded and led by Eleanor Mann with a team of breathwork practitioners. Some sessions count toward the school's own Breathwork Practitioner Training certification. Upcoming Byron Bay retreats are listed for 19–25 April, 12–18 July and 22–28 November 2027, with published rates of AUD $4,750 (twin share) to $5,495 (single room) for the April retreat, including meals; the school also lists retreats in Canada, the Netherlands and Portugal.
