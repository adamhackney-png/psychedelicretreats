Amanda Oliver is the director and founder of Integrate Therapy & Consulting, a counselling and psychotherapy practice based in West Perth, WA, with additional visits to regional WA and telehealth sessions available. The practice's site states Amanda holds a Masters of Counselling & Psychotherapy and a Graduate Diploma in Psychological Science, and that she has completed a Certificate in Psychedelic Assisted Therapy.

The practice's psychedelic-assisted-therapy page describes psychological preparation and integration support for people considering, undertaking or reflecting on a psychedelic-assisted treatment or other altered-state experience, in sessions it describes as client-centred, non-interpretive, trauma-informed and harm-reduction informed. The page states: "Psychotherapy only. We do not prescribe, supply or administer psychedelic medicines." This listing covers preparation and integration support only, not a dosing, facilitation, or supply service.

Published fees at the time of review (11 November 2026) for psychedelic preparation and integration were $250 for a 50-minute session (online or in person at the West Perth office) and $150 for a 30-minute online session.

Psychedelic integration talk therapy involves no controlled substances and is legal throughout Australia. This is not medical advice.
