EPIC Healing Eugene (Eugene Psychedelic Integrative Center) is a psilocybin service centre in Eugene, Oregon, operating within the state-regulated Oregon Psilocybin Services framework. Local coverage in May 2023 reported that the Oregon Health Authority issued the state's first psilocybin service centre licence to EPIC Healing Eugene. Facilitation is provided by its sister company, Radiant Heart Consulting, whose licensed facilitators also work from a partner service centre in Portland.

This is a day-session model rather than a residential retreat. Sessions run one-on-one, for couples, or for small groups of up to six people who know each other. The operator states that each private session includes three hours of individual preparation and two hours of individual integration, and that clients can also join free online preparation and integration groups. Intake starts with a call in which the operator asks the state-required screening questions; for questions about medications or medical conditions, it refers clients to their own medical professionals, as it is not a medical setting.

The operator links to published price sheets. Facilitation for a single high-dose session is USD $2,500 with a licensed facilitator or $2,800 with a senior-level facilitator for one client (approx. <AudPrice usd={2500} usdHigh={2800} />). Lower per-person rates apply to groups, and two-session packages are also listed. The psilocybin product is charged separately, in cash, at USD $16 per mg including Oregon's 15% psilocybin tax (for example, $400 for a 25 mg dose). At the Portland partner centre, product pricing and room fees differ.

This service operates in Oregon, USA, where psilocybin services are legal for adults 21+ at licensed service centres under a state-regulated program. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
