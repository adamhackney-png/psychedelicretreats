Retraite Éveil runs 3-day and 5-day residential psilocybin-truffle retreats near Amsterdam in small groups, with a stated ratio of one facilitator per three participants, plus a private "VIP" 3-day option. The operator runs retreats in French as well as English, with Spanish and Ukrainian also listed on its site. Programs include preparation sessions, music-supported ceremony, structured integration sessions and a personal integration call 15 days after the retreat, with ongoing community access via an app.

The operator states that it uses exclusively psilocybin truffles, "the only legal form in the Netherlands", and explicitly notes that it does not offer psychedelic therapy and that psilocybin is not a medicine. Applicants complete a health questionnaire with contraindications screened before acceptance — its published exclusions include diagnosed schizophrenia, bipolar disorder or borderline personality disorder (or close family history of these), current serotonin-acting medication, and pregnancy or breastfeeding — and the operator states it refuses applications where health poses risks. Participation is 18+. On safety, the operator states that a health professional is present during ceremony, that an experienced team is on site to manage difficult moments, and that the retreat centre is roughly 25 minutes from the nearest hospital.

As of August 2026 the operator publishes pricing on its site: the 3-day "Discovery Immersion" listed from €1,490, the 5-day "In-Depth Immersion" from €2,990, and a private 3-day "VIP" option from €12,900 — each shown as a discount against a higher standard rate — with upcoming August 2026 dates displayed.

This program is held in the Netherlands, where psilocybin truffles are legal to sell and possess. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
