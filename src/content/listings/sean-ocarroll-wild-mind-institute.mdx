Sean O'Carroll is a Melbourne-based psychotherapist whose practice, Wild Mind Institute, offers individual psychotherapy, psychedelic integration support — including a stated specialisation in integrating difficult ("bad trip") experiences — eco-psychotherapy, and supervision and training for other practitioners, in person and online.

His site states that he has served as Head of Psychotherapy at Clarion Clinics in Melbourne, as a supervisor, lead psychotherapist and trainer with the Monash University Clinical Psychedelics Research Lab, and as a lead therapist and trainer on the Swinburne University psilocybin trial for treatment-resistant depression, and that he completed MAPS training in MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD and has lectured in transpersonal psychology for more than ten years.

The site states explicitly: "I do not offer underground psychedelic-assisted psychotherapy." This listing is for integration and psychotherapy support only — not a dosing, facilitation, or supply service.
