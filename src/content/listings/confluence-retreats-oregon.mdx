Confluence Retreats runs residential psilocybin retreats near Ashland, Oregon, built around the state-regulated Oregon Psilocybin Services framework. The psilocybin sessions themselves take place at Omnia Group Ashland, a licensed psilocybin service centre that the operator names as its partner; an Oregon Health Authority order from May 2026 confirms Omnia Group Ashland, LLC has held service-centre licence SC-4372d137 since August 2023. Confluence says it is stewarded by the nonprofit Hi-U Foundation.

The main program is a 5-day, 4-night group retreat for up to 8 people, with a lower-dose group session on day two and a higher-dose session on day four, plus breathwork, meditation, forest walks and meals. Accommodation is forest cabins in the Cascade-Siskiyou National Monument outside Ashland, or a hilltop house above Ashland from December to March. Preparation starts two to four weeks before, with online group workshops and a 90-minute session with a facilitator; integration runs four to six weeks after, with online group workshops and a 1:1 session. The operator also offers a 3-day private retreat with one high-dose session, and a one-day session in Ashland without lodging.

The operator states that its facilitators are Oregon-licensed and that applications are reviewed individually, taking into account psychological and medical history and current medication. Under Oregon's program, participants must be 21 or over. The founder, Myles Katz, is described on the site as having held an Oregon psilocybin facilitator licence since April 2024.

Published pricing at the time of review was USD $6,400 for the 5-day group retreat (approx. <AudPrice usd={6400} />), all-inclusive except flights to Medford; $4,900 for the 3-day private retreat; and $3,200 for the one-day session including centre and psilocybin fees. Listed 5-day dates ran from 9–13 December 2026 through 9–13 June 2027, as reviewed on 22 November 2026.

This service operates in Oregon, USA, where psilocybin services are legal for adults 21+ at licensed service centres under a state-regulated program. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
