Rise Temple Retreat runs private breathwork and meditation retreats and sessions on a 45-acre property in Upper Burringbar, in the Northern Rivers hinterland of NSW near Byron Bay. Its main retreat format, "Return to Stillness", is a private two-night weekend (Friday 4:30pm to Sunday 1:00pm) for one guest or for a couple. These are not group retreats. Each weekend includes private accommodation, breathwork, guided meditation, time in nature, sauna, yoga, massage and meals. The operator states that no previous breathwork or meditation experience is needed. Extra nights can be arranged. The site also offers private 1:1 and couples sessions in person and online, a 5-week "Breath & Belief Reset" program, and an online practice library.

A dedicated NDIS-support page on the site states that Rise Temple is not a registered NDIS provider, but that it works with self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants through invoicing and documentation.

The founder and facilitator, Amanda Jane, describes herself on the site as a meditation, breathwork and consciousness guide whose practice includes multiple 10-day Vipassana retreats and the study of Advaita Vedanta.

Breathwork involves no controlled substances and is legal throughout Australia. This is not medical advice; people with cardiovascular or other significant health conditions should seek advice from a qualified health professional before participating.

The Return to Stillness weekend is published at AUD $2,333 for one guest and AUD $2,888 per couple. Retreat payments are non-refundable but can be transferred to another date within 12 months, according to the site's booking policy.
