AYA Healing Retreats runs ayahuasca retreats at El Encanto de Chullachaqui, a jungle centre in the Loreto region about an hour and 45 minutes' drive from Iquitos, followed by a short walk in. The operator says it was founded in 2017, and its site names Elio Geusa as founder. Ceremonies are led by Shipibo practitioners; the 7-day program is led by Maestro Don Miguel, who the site says was born in the Shipibo community of Roaboya on the Ucayali River and has led ceremonies at the centre since 2012.

The 7-day, 6-night program combines two Shipibo ayahuasca ceremonies (days two and four) with a full-day San Pedro (huachuma) ceremony on day five, led by a separate facilitator the operator does not name publicly. The schedule also includes a personal consultation with the maestro, an icaros class, vapour and flower baths, a cacao ceremony, integration circles and a closing fire ceremony. Groups are capped at 12, each participant has a private bungalow with a private bathroom, and ground transfers from Iquitos are included. Ceremonies are translated from Spanish and Shipibo into English. The operator also runs longer formats at the same centre: an 11-day ayahuasca and master plant dieta, a 26-day "Shamanic Initiation", an 11-day Noya Rao dieta with voice classes and a 13-day women's retreat.

Applicants complete a pre-application, then a full psychological and physical health questionnaire that the operator says was developed with ICEERS (the International Center for Ethnobotanical Education, Research and Service), followed by a call. The site states that a Shipibo practitioner, two English-speaking facilitators and a ceremonial assistant attend each ceremony, and lists a first-aid kit, 24-hour emergency transport to a local clinic, outside assistance from medical staff in Iquitos and an overnight sitter after ceremonies. It flags that many antidepressants and some psychiatric and heart conditions are incompatible with ayahuasca. The homepage displays the operator's Google reviews.

As of November 2026 the operator publishes a full price of USD $1,850 for the 7-day program (approx. <AudPrice usd={1850} />), with an early-bird rate of $1,650 for the first four bookings of each date, and reduced rates for returning participants and residents of Central and South America. A $550 deposit confirms a place after acceptance. Flights, Iquitos accommodation before and after the retreat, and travel insurance (which the operator requires) are not included. Listed 2027 dates for the 7-day program were 2–8 August and 14–20 August, as reviewed on 25 November 2026.

This program is held in Peru, where traditional ayahuasca and San Pedro (huachuma) ceremony is legal and culturally protected (see our Peru destination guide). Ayahuasca (which contains DMT) and San Pedro (which contains mescaline) are prohibited substances in Australia. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
