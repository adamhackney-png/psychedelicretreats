Beckley Retreats' Jamaica program runs the same eleven-week structure as its Netherlands program — around four weeks of virtual preparation, a 5–6 day in-person retreat, and roughly six weeks of integration support — held at Good Hope Estate, about 45 minutes from Montego Bay's airport.

Psilocybin mushrooms were never scheduled under Jamaica's Dangerous Drugs Act, and Beckley's own materials state this explicitly. Facilitator bios published by the program include Head of Research Dr AZA Allsop, and the program describes a similar ~1:3 facilitator ratio to its other locations. Beckley's current retreat pages describe the Jamaica Signature Program as 5 nights with two guided psilocybin sessions, for up to 18 guests, and Good Hope as a restored 2,000-acre estate with thirteen rooms with their own bathrooms.

Published pricing is USD $5,500–$11,000 per guest (approx. <AudPrice usd={5500} usdHigh={11000} />) depending on room type, with shared rooms at the lower end; flights and on-site massage are not included.
