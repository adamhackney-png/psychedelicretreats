Suzanne Herrity offers one-on-one preparation and integration sessions for people around psychedelic experiences, from Selby in Melbourne's Dandenong Ranges and online Australia-wide and internationally. This is support before and after an experience only — her site states she supports clients "before and after your experience rather than being present during it", and notes that psychedelic-assisted psychotherapy is currently available in Australia only in specific authorised clinical settings. This is not a dosing, facilitation or supply service.

Her site describes her as a PACFA-accredited therapist working in somatic psychotherapy, Internal Family Systems (IFS), Somatic IFS and Advanced Integrative Therapy, with psychedelic-specific training listed including Mind Medicine Australia's Fundamentals of Psychedelic Psychotherapy course, IFS-and-psychedelics trainings with Bob Falconer and Nancy Morgan, somatics-and-psychedelics study with The Embody Lab, and ethics training with Kylea Taylor. The approach is described as trauma-informed and grounded in harm reduction and nervous-system awareness, with around six sessions typically recommended.

Published pricing is AUD $170 per session including GST, with a free 30-minute initial consultation.
