Willka T'ika is a garden retreat centre about three kilometres from the town of Urubamba in Peru's Sacred Valley, between Cusco and Machu Picchu. Founded by Carol Cumes and operating since the mid-1990s, it hosts visiting group retreats, personal stays and its own in-house programs, with up to 65 guests in 35 rooms set around seven themed "Chakra Gardens". The operator describes itself as a luxury wellness retreat and states plainly that it is "a holistic wellness retreat rather than a plant-medicine center".

The program relevant to this directory is the 7-day Essential Wellness Retreat, which the operator runs on set dates several times a year. Each day is built around one of the seven gardens and combines yoga, vegetarian meals, massage and spa sessions, hikes and Andean ceremonial practices led by a visiting Q'ero practitioner, including a despacho offering, a k'intu blessing, a fire ceremony, coca-leaf reading and a cacao ceremony. On day six, participants can choose an optional San Pedro (huachuma) ceremony, a full-day ceremony led by the centre's resident shaman, or an alternative visit to an Incan cave site.

Willka T'ika says San Pedro is the only psychoactive plant it offers, and only to people taking the full 7-day program, because the earlier days serve as preparation. It does not offer ayahuasca, and it does not allow guests or visiting groups to run their own ceremonies with ayahuasca, San Pedro or other psychoactive substances on the property. Private San Pedro ceremonies outside the retreat are possible only with advance notice, a completed medical questionnaire and a stay of at least three nights. For general medical care, the operator says it works with a private travel clinic in the area whose ambulance can reach the property in about 20 minutes, and that it keeps a small oxygen tank on site.

Published pricing at the time of review was USD $2,470 for the 7-day Essential Wellness Retreat (approx. <AudPrice usd={2470} />), with the San Pedro ceremony an extra USD $250. Listed 2027 dates were 12–18 March, 18–24 June, 13–19 August and 12–18 November, as reviewed on 25 November 2026. The operator's reviews page shows Google reviews and links to its TripAdvisor listing.

This program is held in Peru, where traditional San Pedro (huachuma) ceremony is legal (see our Peru destination guide). San Pedro contains mescaline, which is a prohibited substance in Australia. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
