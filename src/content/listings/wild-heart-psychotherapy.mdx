Wild Heart Psychotherapy is a Melbourne-based practice offering individual psychotherapy and counselling from a clinic in Brunswick, with additional sessions on the Surfcoast at Lorne, and via telehealth. Its services include psychedelic integration support — working with clients to process non-ordinary states of consciousness they experienced independently — alongside general psychotherapy, men's groups, and Holotropic Breathwork workshops.

The practice's approach draws on Gestalt, process-oriented, and transpersonal psychotherapy. Its psychotherapist trained in Holotropic Breathwork through the Grof Transpersonal Facilitator Training and became a certified Holotropic Breathwork practitioner in 2013.

This listing covers psychotherapy, integration, and breathwork services only — not a dosing, facilitation, or supply service.
