Rose Waters Sanctuary describes itself as a licensed psilocybin service centre in Portland, Oregon, operating within the state-regulated Oregon Psilocybin Services framework. It offers individual psilocybin sessions, group ceremonies and 5-day retreats for women. The operator states that all psilocybin administration takes place at the service centre, at 840 SE Washington St, Portland, while retreat accommodation is in a separate private residence.

The women's retreat runs over five days and four nights: an opening circle on day one, a low-dose group session on day two, a higher-dose group session on day three, an integration day on day four and a closing circle on day five, with meditation, yoga and chef-prepared meals throughout. Past retreat participants are offered a free monthly online integration call. Participants do not need to be Oregon residents, but Oregon's program requires them to be 21 or over.

The FAQ states that "Oregon law requires that all clients complete a health screening with a licensed facilitator before any administration session." The team page lists a large roster of Oregon-licensed psilocybin facilitators, with bios written by the operator.

Published retreat pricing at the time of review was USD $3,300 for a shared bunk room, $3,900 for a single room with shared bathroom and $4,400 for a single room with private bathroom (approx. <AudPrice usd={3300} usdHigh={4400} />); the January 2027 retreat was listed from $3,200. The operator puts an individual session, with preparation and integration, at roughly $2,000 to $3,500 depending on the facilitator. Listed retreat dates were 13–17 January, 24–28 February and 14–18 April 2027, as reviewed on 22 November 2026.

This service operates in Oregon, USA, where psilocybin services are legal for adults 21+ at licensed service centres under a state-regulated program. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
