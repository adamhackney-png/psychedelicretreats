Earth Awareness is a Dutch foundation (Stichting Earth Awareness) that runs psilocybin-truffle retreats at its NatuurTempel, a round, hand-built wood, glass and clay building on an off-grid site with a vegetable garden, near Twello. The foundation says it was set up in 2006; it does not own the land and uses part of each ticket price to save towards buying it. The site is in Dutch, but group activities are held in English and the team also speaks Dutch.

The main formats are a 3-day weekend retreat (Friday to Sunday) with one truffle ceremony with live music, preceded by breathwork, yoga and a medicine-wheel ceremony; and a 5-day retreat with two truffle ceremonies, daily workshops, a 1:1 coaching session and an integration workshop. A shorter 2-day retreat without workshops and private ceremonies are also offered. Group retreats run from April to October. The stated maximum group size is 24 for the 3-day retreat and 15 for the 5-day retreat. On the 3-day retreat participants sleep in the temple unless they book a private tent on the covered indoor campsite; showers and toilets are outdoors and off-grid. Meals are vegetarian.

The team page names founders Mark and Maya, who the operator says began working with psychedelics around 30 years ago and have run retreats together since 2008. Booking starts with an intake form. The operator excludes people taking antidepressants, antipsychotics or certain other medication, and people with heart conditions, epilepsy or other serious health problems unless their doctor agrees, and asks participants to disclose medical and psychological conditions in the intake. It also states that its facilitators are experienced but have no formal medical training.

On legality, the operator states (in Dutch) that in the Netherlands magic mushrooms are illegal but the truffles (sclerotia) are fully legal. Published pricing at the time of review was €460 for the 3-day retreat, with a private tent at €50 and a station pick-up at €21 extra, and €1,450 for the 5-day retreat. Listed 2027 dates were 9–11 April, 4–6 June, 18–20 June, 9–11 July, 17–19 September and 1–3 October (3-day) and 13–17 May, 22–26 July and 2–6 September (5-day), as reviewed on 22 November 2026.

This program is held in the Netherlands, where psilocybin truffles are legal to sell and possess. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
