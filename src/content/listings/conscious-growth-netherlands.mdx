Conscious Growth runs 3-day weekend psilocybin-truffle retreats in the Netherlands, from Friday afternoon to Sunday lunch, for groups of up to 12. Each retreat has one medium-to-high-dose truffle session on the Saturday, with individual and group preparation beforehand, and somatic, creative and integration practices around it. The operator says it uses a selection of rural venues within a 2 to 3-hour train ride of Amsterdam, with shared rooms of 2 to 4 beds and vegetarian meals, and includes transport from a nearby train station. Retreats are run in English.

Before the retreat there is an introductory call, a registration form with a medical-history section, a consultation with a lead facilitator and an online group preparation meeting. After it, the operator offers in-person support in Amsterdam in the first hours after the retreat, phone support in the following week, a personal integration call and an online group integration meeting one to two weeks later. Its FAQ lists medication and health contraindications and says a doctor's letter may be requested in some cases. The operator states that its venues are 20 to 30 minutes' drive from a hospital, and that it "does not offer therapy or treatment for physical or mental health conditions during the retreats."

The team page names co-founders and lead facilitators Joan Sirera and Michael Conti, whom the operator describes as therapists, together with other facilitators. Their bios are the operator's own.

On legality, the operator states: "In the Netherlands it is legal to buy and use magic truffles (psilocybin truffles)." Published pricing at the time of review was €1,850, or €1,757 with a 5% early-bird discount up to a month before, including board, lodging, the preparation and integration sessions and taxes. The operator states that "for legal reasons, the truffles are not included in the retreat price." A supporter price funds scholarship places. The next listed retreat was 19–21 March 2027, as reviewed on 22 November 2026.

This program is held in the Netherlands, where psilocybin truffles are legal to sell and possess. Psilocybin remains a prohibited substance in Australia outside the authorised clinical pathway. Australians travel at their own responsibility; this is not medical advice.
